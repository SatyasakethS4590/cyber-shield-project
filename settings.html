Privacy & safety

  • Never share your password or one-time codes.
  • Use unique passwords and enable multifactor authentication where available.
  • Practise only on systems you own or have explicit permission to test.
  • CyberShield's local tutor is a topic guide, not a live AI service.
Account deletion, billing changes and password recovery should be handled through Supabase Auth/provider controls; this page does not pretend to implement those actions.
